Beyond the estimate

More ways Backblaze reduces storage costs

The calculator models standard list pricing. These programs can push your real-world bill lower still.

Switch without the migration costs

Moving petabytes doesn't have to mean a costly migration project. Backblaze supports partner tools, transfer services, and subsidized migration programs for qualifying workloads to cut the cost and complexity of leaving on-prem or another cloud.

Explore Universal Data Migration

Your real costs could be even lower

This estimate assumes standard B2 pricing. If you use an eligible CDN, compute, or networking partner, the Backblaze Bandwidth Alliance eliminates data-transfer fees between participating partners, potentially dropping your egress to zero.

Explore the Bandwidth Alliance

Replicate data without transfer fees

B2 Cloud Replication automatically copies data to another bucket, account, or region for redundancy, compliance, disaster recovery, and faster local access. Replication has no service or egress fees; standard B2 storage rates apply to the copy.
